Every palette has the same structure: the sidebar and conversation share the window ground (`--bg`), and the panel, sheets, cards and composer are planes one step off it (`--surface`). Product surfaces stay flat (no gradient). Preserve distinctions between action fills, navigation selection, links, and status colors instead of applying a brand color to all of them. Metadata and placeholders use a readable text role; supporting text is checked at 4.5:1 against the canvas, sidebar, composer, and raised surfaces. Disabled states and decorative marks remain separate.

## Adaptations for the shared ground (October 20, 2026)

The conversation now sits on the sidebar's ground, so a palette whose sidebar and canvas were one light color gets a hand-tuned ground step, and its fills move one step down so a hovered row still shows on the ground:

- Codex light: ground `#f5f5f5`, fills `#ececec` / `#e5e5e5`, bubble white; muted `#646464`, accent text `#0163c0`.
- Linear light: ground `lch(96.5% 0.9 282)`, fills `lch(94% 1 282)` / `lch(91.5% 1.1 282)`, bubble on the canvas color; accent text `lch(42.5% 70 286.91)`.
- Halcyon light: ground `#eff2f7`, fills `#e7ebf2` / `#dfe4ed`, bubble `#f8f9fc`; gold text `#895900`.
- Kamakura light: ground `#eae8dd`, fills `#e0e2d4` / `#d7dacb`, bubble `#f5f3eb`; muted `#565e4c`.
- Cyberpunk light: ground `#e8eaf4`, fills `#dde1ee` / `#d3d8e8`, bubble `#f3f4fa`; muted `#545d7a`, accent text `#006776`.
- Eliron light: ground `#f5f7f8`, fills `#eceff1` / `#e3e7ea`.

Palettes that already had a step keep it, with these changes:

- Claude light: the canvas `#f9f9f7` becomes the ground and planes turn white, as in Claude's composer; fills `#f0efec` / `#e8e7e3`; muted `#696763`, faint `#6e6c67`, clay text `#a64d2d`.
- Conductor light: fills `#f5f5f4` / `#eeedeb` (the old fill equaled the ground); white writing area and bubble; muted `#6f6967`.
- GitHub light: fills `#eff2f5` / `#e9edf1`, white bubble; green text `#187734`.
- Dark: Codex muted `#8e8e8e`, Linear muted `lch(57.5% 1.2 272)`, Claude muted `#9a9890` and clay text `#dc8163`, Halcyon muted `#99a6c3`.
- Status defaults in dark are brighter (`#3fae74`, `#c0973a`, `#e27d6f`) so they hold 4.5:1 on their soft fills in every dark palette. `--bad` and `--bad-soft` are palette keys now, and `--attention` (status dots) defaults to `--warn`.

Paper, the writing area and the bubble default to the plane (`--surface`) unless a palette paints them. Every pair is checked by `.local-development/redesign/tools/contrast.ts`.

When updating a reference, record the source and date, inspect both appearances, and verify saved color overrides. Customizing one palette must not change another.
