# Artwork provenance

The original app icon, OpenOrc mark, avatars, Rive mascot, and rocket are distributed under the project's Apache-2.0 terms. This document identifies the source assets and their derivations.

Provider marks, fonts, and third-party runtime code retain their own terms. Original-art ownership does not grant rights to those external assets or their trademarks.

[artwork-manifest.json](artwork-manifest.json) fingerprints the image, SVG, Rive, and icon files curated for the public source. It includes provider marks to identify their bytes, not to classify them as original work. A hash establishes bytes, not ownership or permission; refresh the record when an asset changes.

## Onboarding illustrations

The three flat illustrations in `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/assets/onboarding/` are original images generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on November 9, 2026. They depict abstract ribbons, a graphic flower, and interlocking shapes. No third-party reference images were supplied. Each image is distributed as a 640 × 640 WebP; the adjacent [provenance record](../apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/assets/onboarding/provenance.json) contains the exact prompts, encoding details, and SHA-256 fingerprints. These illustrations are distributed under the project's Apache-2.0 terms. Existing provider marks rendered beside them retain their separate terms.

## Project framework and language logos

The 24 SVGs in `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/assets/project-stacks/` are copied byte-for-byte from [Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/54cfe13ac10eaa1ef817a343ab0a9437eb3c2e08), pinned to commit `54cfe13ac10eaa1ef817a343ab0a9437eb3c2e08`. The adjacent `manifest.json` records each source URL, file size and SHA-256. Use upstream logos for these identifiers; the user explicitly rejected original OpenOrc drawings for framework/language marks.

These assets retain [Devicon's MIT copyright and license notice](../licenses/vendor/devicon-MIT.txt), which is included in desktop distributions. Product names and logos belong to their respective owners and identify the detected technology; this does not imply endorsement. They are not original OpenOrc artwork. The renderer keeps upstream geometry and colors, adds a light ground behind Astro/Django for contrast, and inverts only monochrome Express/Rust in dark appearance. No SVG files are modified by these display treatments.

## Platform marks

The website's Download button names the visitor's platform with its mark (`apps/website/src/lib/platform-icons.ts`). The Apple and Linux paths are copied from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), whose icon data is CC0 1.0; the Linux mark is a silhouette of Tux, whose original artwork is by Larry Ewing. The Windows mark is drawn as four squares. The marks identify the platform an installer is for and remain their owners' trademarks; they do not imply endorsement.

Keep compatibility statements factual and separate from partnership claims. Review the app, website, source SVGs and screenshots together if replacing a provider mark. Attribution or a non-endorsement statement does not replace any required permission.
