Product surfaces stay flat. Preserve distinctions between action fills, navigation selection, links, and status colors instead of applying a brand color to all of them. Metadata and placeholders use a readable text role; supporting text is checked at 4.5:1 against the canvas, sidebar, composer, and raised surfaces. Disabled states and decorative marks remain separate.

When updating a reference, record the source and date, inspect both appearances, and verify saved color overrides. OpenOrc and Cursor must continue to have equal default tokens and typography; customization of one must not change the other.
